SEC-01 // ROLE AND CONTEXT

Role and context

I joined the PSF Instru team at Reliance Industries Limited in Navi Mumbai as a summer intern for two months, working on-site in a live plant environment rather than on a simulation.

The instrumentation and automation function sits between the field devices and the software that runs on them, which is exactly the boundary I want to work on: controllers, networks, and the maintenance practices that keep both trustworthy.

My work was a study rather than a commissioning role — observing how things are done today, understanding why, and proposing an improvement with the constraints of a running plant in mind.

SEC-02 // INTERNSHIP PROJECT

Study Existing Software/Program Storage Methods and Suggest Improvement

SCOPE OF STUDY

The study looked at how software and control programs are stored, updated and kept recoverable on an industrial control system — the practical question of where the "known good" copy of everything lives, and how it gets there.

Four areas were covered:

  • Software patching — how updates are identified and applied on control-system software.
  • Update storage — where patch files and update packages are held before and after deployment.
  • Backup — how program and configuration backups are taken and verified.
  • Lifecycle management — keeping track of versions and revisions across the life of a system.

SEC-03 // CONCEPTS STUDIED

Concepts studied

ARCHITECTURE
  • Rockwell PlantPAx DCS architecture — how a distributed control system is laid out in practice.
  • The Purdue Model as the reference for separating levels of an industrial control system.
  • Plant network and control network architecture, and why they are kept distinct.
INFRASTRUCTURE
  • Domain controllers and their role in authenticating users and machines on the network.
  • Historian — the long-term record of process data, and why its integrity matters.
  • NAS storage — where backups and software packages are kept, and how it is reached.

SEC-04 // NETWORKING & CYBERSECURITY EXPOSURE

Industrial networking and cybersecurity exposure

Alongside the main study I was exposed to the network security side of the plant: how traffic between levels is controlled, where the boundary between networks sits, and what devices enforce it.

  • Firewalls — where they sit and what rules they exist to enforce.
  • DMZ — the buffer zone between plant networks and everything above them.
  • Network switches — the physical layer everything else depends on.
